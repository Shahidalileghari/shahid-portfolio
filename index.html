My approach

Understand first.
Build with care.

A straightforward way of working that keeps the people and the problem in view.

01 · Ask good questions

I start by getting clear on who the software is for, what they need, and what a useful first version could look like.

02 · Make it understandable

I value readable code and clear interfaces—small choices that make a product easier to use and a project easier to maintain.

03 · Improve together

I welcome feedback, test assumptions, and keep learning as the work takes shape. Good results come from collaboration.
